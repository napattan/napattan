## 💡 What I Do

I work at the intersection of **urban design, spatial data, and software engineering**. 

Rather than treating architecture and technology as separate worlds, I build tools that help design teams and city planners make smarter, data-driven decisions:
- **Analyze Big Urban Data**: Turning massive city datasets into interactive maps and actionable planning dashboards.
- **Simulate City Climates**: Modeling urban heat, shade, solar radiation, and human comfort to create resilient public spaces.
- **Automate Design Workflows**: Developing custom Python/C# tools and AI integrations that eliminate tedious manual modeling.
